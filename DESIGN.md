## Brand & Style

This design system embodies mindful tranquility, refined warmth, and organic luxury for a boutique wellness and aesthetics sanctuary. The visual language evokes the sensation of stepping into an airy, sun-dappled spa suite: deeply grounding, unhurried, and intimate. 

The stylistic direction blends warm minimalism with delicate editorial elegance. Generous negative space acts as visual breathing room, allowing rich typography and tactile warmth to take center stage. Sharp corners and harsh contrasts are avoided in favor of soft contours, sun-bleached earthy undertones, and gossamer-fine golden accents. The interface feels reassuring, bespoke, and profoundly relaxing.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in Mediterranean earth, natural linens, and radiant skin tones:

- **Primary (`#704B4B` - Deep Chestnut / Marrón Profundo):** Provides grounding contrast and authority for prominent headings, focal calls-to-action, and primary active states.
- **Secondary (`#B98169` - Terracotta Rose / Terracota Rosado):** Warm and comforting; applied to decorative flourishes, badge highlights, active tab indicators, and contextual icons.
- **Tertiary (`#C9A97A` - Champagne Gold / Dorado Champán):** An opulent yet understated metallic accent used for delicate divider lines, star ratings, subtle borders, and special badges.
- **Neutral (`#7B5858` - Soft Earth / Marrón Suave):** The foundation for long-form body text, supporting metadata, and field labels, delivering high legibility without the stark harshness of pure black.

### Surface Tiers
- **Canvas Base (`#FBF6F0` - Crema):** The luminous, warm-white foundation across entire viewports.
- **Surface Level 1 (`#FFEFD7` - Light Sand):** Soft container fills for cards, menus, and grouped content.
- **Surface Level 2 (`#FFECCF` - Deep Sand):** Alternating full-width section bands, active selection fills, and hovered surfaces.
- **Surface Border (`rgba(201, 169, 122, 0.25)`): Whispering champagne outlines framing interactive elements.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances classical, high-fashion serif forms with light, airy geometric sans-serif prose:

- **Headlines & Section Titles:** Rendered in `Playfair Display`. Major titles and hero copy can optionally employ tracked uppercase styling (`letterSpacing: 0.08em` to `0.12em`) to mirror luxury boutique stationery.
- **Editorial Flourishes & Accents:** Rendered in italicized `Playfair Display` (`font-style: italic`, `font-weight: 400`) within running headlines or as standalone introductory phrases to bring an intimate, personal salon aesthetic.
- **Body & Structural Elements:** Set in `Montserrat` with light (`300`) and regular (`400`) weights. This delivers crystal-clear legibility for service descriptions, ritual durations, and pricing without competing with the headline serifs.
- **Caps Labels & Badges:** Rendered in `Montserrat` medium (`500`) or semibold (`600`) in uppercase with generous letter spacing to evoke understated fragrance and cosmetics packaging.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around deliberate emptiness and measured balance, giving every aesthetic treatment room to breathe:

- **Grid Architecture:** Desktop views utilize a 12-column responsive fluid grid bounded at a maximum container width of `1200px` to maintain focused readability. Tablet views transition to an 8-column grid (`gutter-sm: 1rem`, `margin-md: 3rem`), while mobile devices use a 4-column flow (`margin: 1.5rem`).
- **Vertical Cadence:** Large vertical spacers (`space-xl` and above) bracket treatment offerings and testimonial blocks. Section-level vertical margins (`margin-lg: 6rem`) separate thematic services, preventing visual compression and preserving an unhurried browsing pace.
- **Alignment:** Centralized symmetry for hero and intro statements transitions smoothly into asymmetric 2-column or 3-column service cards for treatment lists and booking options.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids dark, artificial drop shadows, relying instead on diffused, sun-warmed ambient depth and subtle surface nesting:

- **Ambient Shading:** Shadows are tinted with warm chestnut (`#704B4B`) at extremely low opacity (3% to 6%) with extended blur radii (16px to 32px) and positive vertical displacement (`0 10px 30px rgba(112, 75, 75, 0.05)`). The visual result is floating linen resting gently on silk.
- **Tonal Layering:** Depth is primarily established through chromatic stacking: The canvas (`#FBF6F0`) holds sand cards (`#FFEFD7`), which in turn can host sunken, inset appointment modules or elevated terracotta accents.
- **Hairline Accents:** Separation between adjacent surfaces is achieved with whisper-thin champagne dividers (`rgba(201, 169, 122, 0.25)` to `rgba(201, 169, 122, 0.4)`), delivering tactile definition without jarring contrasts.

## Shapes

The shape philosophy mirrors smooth stones, natural clays, and flowing silhouettes:

- **Global Radii:** Standard components feature soft, approachable rounding (`rounded: 0.5rem` / `8px`).
- **Cards & Modal Containers:** Utilize `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`) and `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / `24px`) to create soothing, organic perimeters that guide the eye naturally inward.
- **Pill Attributes:** Service tags, filter badges, and primary action buttons apply full pill curvature (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to emphasize touch-friendliness and calm comfort.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Action (Book Treatment):** Pill-shaped, filled with Marrón Profundo (`#704B4B`), featuring white or soft crema text in tracked uppercase Montserrat (`label-md`). Hover elevates subtly with a Terracotta Rosado (`#B98169`) shift and soft ambient glow.
- **Secondary Action (Inquire / View Menu):** Pill-shaped with a transparent core, bounded by a 1px Champagne Gold border (`#C9A97A`) and Marrón Profundo label. Hover transitions the background smoothly into `#FFECCF`.
- **Tertiary Text Links:** Rendered in `#704B4B` with an animated terracotta underline that expands from center on hover.

### Cards (Treatments & Rituals)
- Background is tinted in Light Sand (`#FFEFD7`) resting upon the Crema base.
- Corners are curved to `1rem` with a hairline border (`1px solid rgba(201, 169, 122, 0.3)`).
- Card layouts place treatment titles in Playfair Display serif, duration in tracked uppercase (`Montserrat 11px`), and price styled with terracotta warmth. Includes generous internal padding (`space-lg`).

### Form Elements & Inputs
- Text inputs and date pickers use flat `#FBF6F0` or `#FFFFFF` backgrounds bordered by subtle champagne gold strokes (`rgba(201, 169, 122, 0.5)`).
- Padding is spacious (`space-sm` vertical, `space-md` horizontal).
- Focus states introduce an ambient terracotta highlight ring (`rgba(185, 129, 105, 0.2)`) and sharpen the border tone to `#B98169`.

### Selection Controls (Checkbox, Radio, Toggles)
- Custom circular indicators with a thin champagne border. 
- Active state fills with Marrón Profundo (`#704B4B`) centered with an organic dot or hairline checkmark in `#FBF6F0`.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- Fully rounded pills with a `#FFECCF` background, `#7B5858` text, and zero borders in inactive states.
- Active chips shift to Terracotta Rosado (`#B98169`) with soft cream text.

### Specialized Salon Components
- **Ritual Price List Item:** A two-column editorial flex row with treatment title and short description on the left, an optional dotted champagne rule connecting across, and bold serif pricing on the right.
- **Therapist / Space Showcase:** Arched image masks (rounded at top `120px`, square or softly rounded at base) bordered by delicate champagne lines to showcase clinic spaces and treatment rituals.
